## The Multi-Platform Reality

Here's a scenario that plays out in thousands of companies every day:

- Your **headquarters** runs on Google Workspace (Gmail)
- Your **US client** uses Microsoft 365 (Outlook)
- Your **China team** communicates via Feishu (Lark)
- Your **freelance contractor** uses a personal IMAP email

Everyone sends email to each other, but each person lives in their own email silo. There's no single place to see the full picture of a project that spans all these platforms.

## Why This Is a Problem

### 1. Information Fragmentation

When approvals, reports, and updates are scattered across 4 different email platforms, no single person has complete visibility. The project manager has to check multiple inboxes — or worse, rely on others to forward relevant emails.

### 2. Missed Context

Thread A in Gmail references Thread B in Outlook. But you can only see Thread A. You're making decisions with incomplete information.

### 3. Platform-Specific Limitations

Each platform has different capabilities:
- Gmail has great search but limited organizational features
- Outlook has powerful rules but a complex interface
- Feishu integrates well with Chinese business tools but is isolated from Western platforms
- IMAP clients vary wildly in features and reliability

### 4. Compliance Complexity

If your email data lives across 4 different providers in 4 different jurisdictions, demonstrating GDPR or CCPA compliance becomes significantly harder.

## The Solution: One Business Layer, Many Platforms

Flowmail connects to all major email platforms through secure OAuth and IMAP protocols:

| Platform | Connection Method | Features |
|----------|------------------|----------|
| **Gmail** | OAuth 2.0 + Push Notifications | Real-time sync, full API access |
| **Microsoft 365 / Outlook** | Microsoft OAuth + Graph API | Calendar integration, org-level access |
| **Feishu / Lark** | OAuth 2.0 + Event Subscription | Native Chinese business tool support |
| **QQ Mail** | IMAP/SMTP | Wide consumer/SMB coverage in China |
| **163 Mail (NetEase)** | IMAP/SMTP | Enterprise email in China |
| **Any IMAP Server** | IMAP/SMTP | Universal compatibility |

Once connected, Flowmail treats all emails the same — regardless of which platform they came from. An approval request from a Gmail user looks identical to one from an Outlook user.

## How It Works

### Step 1: Connect Your Accounts

In Flowmail's settings, click "Add Connection" and choose your platform. For OAuth-based platforms (Gmail, Outlook, Feishu), you'll be redirected to authorize access. For IMAP, you'll enter your server details.

All credentials are encrypted with **AES-256-GCM** and never stored in plaintext.

### Step 2: Sync Your Email

Flowmail pulls your recent emails and starts classifying them. New emails are synced automatically via push notifications (Gmail, Feishu) or periodic polling (IMAP).

### Step 3: See Everything in One Place

Your Smart Inbox shows emails from all connected accounts, organized by business intent — not by which email platform they came from.

## Real-World Use Case: Cross-Border Project

A Chinese tech company working with a US client:

1. **Feishu**: Internal team discusses the project in Chinese
2. **Gmail**: Project manager communicates with the US client in English
3. **Outlook**: The US client's legal team sends contract amendments

Without Flowmail, the project manager juggles three apps. With Flowmail:

- All approval requests surface in one Approvals dashboard
- Weekly reports from both sides auto-structure into a unified timeline
- Deadline alerts fire regardless of which platform the original email came from

## Security Across Platforms

Connecting multiple email accounts raises legitimate security questions. Here's how Flowmail handles it:

- **OAuth tokens are encrypted** at rest with AES-256-GCM
- **Each connection is scoped** — Flowmail only requests the minimum permissions needed
- **Connections can be revoked** at any time from the settings page
- **Audit logs track** every sync, send, and access event per connection
- **Data stays in your organization** — no cross-tenant data sharing

## Getting Started

Connecting your first email account takes about 30 seconds. Most teams start with their primary business email (usually Gmail or Outlook), then add additional platforms as needed.

The goal isn't to replace any of these platforms — it's to give you a unified business layer that works across all of them.
