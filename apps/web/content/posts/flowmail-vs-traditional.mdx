## Not Another Email Client

Let's be clear upfront: **Flowmail is not an email client**. We're not trying to replace Gmail, Outlook, or any other email app. You'll still use your existing email for reading, writing, and sending messages.

Flowmail is something different — it's a **business execution layer** that sits on top of your inbox.

## The Key Differences

### Traditional Email Clients

| Aspect | How It Works |
|--------|-------------|
| **Organization** | Manual folders, labels, and rules |
| **Status tracking** | None — you have to remember what's pending |
| **Cross-platform** | Each client is its own silo |
| **Automation** | Basic rules (if subject contains X, move to Y) |
| **Intelligence** | Spam filter, priority inbox (basic) |

Traditional email clients are designed for **communication** — reading and writing messages. They're great at that job.

### Flowmail

| Aspect | How It Works |
|--------|-------------|
| **Organization** | AI auto-classifies by business intent |
| **Status tracking** | Every item has a status (pending → approved → done) |
| **Cross-platform** | Gmail + Outlook + Feishu + IMAP in one view |
| **Automation** | AI extracts fields, tracks deadlines, sends alerts |
| **Intelligence** | Understands business context (legal, finance, PM, etc.) |

Flowmail is designed for **execution** — turning email content into structured, trackable, actionable business items.

## How They Work Together

The best analogy is **GitHub and your code editor**. You write code in VS Code (your email client), but you track issues, PRs, and deployments in GitHub (Flowmail). They serve different purposes and work together.

Here's what the workflow looks like:

1. **You receive an email in Gmail/Outlook** as usual
2. **Flowmail syncs it** via secure OAuth connection
3. **AI classifies it**: approval request, status report, issue, etc.
4. **Structured data is extracted**: deadline, amount, requester, blockers
5. **You take action in Flowmail**: approve, track, assign, follow up
6. **Flowmail sends the response** via your connected email account

At no point do you need to stop using your existing email. Flowmail simply adds a structured layer on top.

## When to Use What

**Use your email client when you want to:**
- Write and send messages
- Read newsletters and announcements
- Have conversational threads
- Manage personal email

**Use Flowmail when you want to:**
- See all pending approvals in one place
- Track project progress across vendors
- Get alerted before deadlines slip
- View structured timelines of business items
- Manage cross-platform email from one dashboard

## What About Other Tools?

### vs. Task Management (Jira, Linear, Asana)

Task tools are great for internal teams, but they don't work across company boundaries. You can't assign a Jira ticket to your client. Flowmail bridges this gap by extracting tasks from email — the one communication channel that works across organizations.

### vs. CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot)

CRMs track customer relationships. Flowmail tracks business execution. There's overlap, but Flowmail is focused on the operational side: approvals, reports, and issues — not sales pipelines.

### vs. Email Automation (Zapier, Make)

Automation tools can move emails between systems, but they don't *understand* email content. Flowmail's AI reads the email and extracts meaning, which is fundamentally different from rule-based automation.

## The Bottom Line

If you're happy with how you manage email today, you probably don't need Flowmail. But if you've ever:

- Missed an approval deadline because it was buried in your inbox
- Spent 20 minutes searching for "that email from the vendor about the budget"
- Wished you could see all pending items across Gmail and Outlook in one view

Then Flowmail might be exactly what you need. And the best part? You don't have to change anything about how you use email today.
